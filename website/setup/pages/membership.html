Questions

How do you know if I've been a member before?

We check your email against our membership records. If you've joined before under a different email, please use that one so your records stay together.

Can I pay by bank transfer or cash?

Yes. Choose bank transfer at checkout and we'll email our bank details. Your membership is confirmed once the Membership Secretary sees the payment. You can also pay cash at a meeting.

Can I come along before joining?

Yes. Come to a Development Session and say hello. Email mail@buryrefs.org.uk if you'd like someone to look out for you.
