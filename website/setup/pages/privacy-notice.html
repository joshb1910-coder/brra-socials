This notice explains what personal information the Bury & Rochdale Referees' Association ("the RA", "we") collects, why, and what you can ask us to do with it. The RA is run by volunteers, and our Secretary looks after data protection for the committee. Questions go to mail@buryrefs.org.uk.

What we collect

  • When you join or renew: your name, email address, and, if you give them, your phone number, FA number, refereeing level and County FA. We also keep the dates and type of your membership.
  • When you pay: payments are taken by Stripe on its own secure page. We see the amount, the date and the last four digits of the card, but never your full card details.
  • When you say you're coming to an event: your name and email address.
  • When you email us: whatever you choose to tell us.
  • At our events: we sometimes take photos for our website and social media. Tell us on the night or by email if you'd rather not appear, and we'll remove any photo of you on request.

Why we use it

  • To run your membership: taking your subscription, keeping the membership list, sending you notices of meetings and the AGM, and reminding you when it's time to renew. We need this to provide your membership.
  • To check whether you've been a member before, so first-time members get the reduced price. We keep past members' email addresses for this, which is in the RA's legitimate interests.
  • To plan events and to answer your questions.
  • To keep proper financial records, which the RA's rules and the law require.

We don't sell your information, and we don't use it for advertising.

Who else sees it

Only committee members who need it for their role, such as the Membership Secretary and the Treasurer. We also use a few trusted services to run the RA: Stripe for payments, Hostinger to host this website, and our email provider. Each of these only handles your information to provide that service to us.

How long we keep it

We keep your membership details while you're a member. After you leave, we keep payment records for six years for our accounts, and your email address so we know you've been a member before. Event sign-ups are only kept until the end of that season. Ask us at any time and we'll delete anything we don't need to keep.

Cookies

This website only uses the cookies it needs to work, such as keeping you logged in to your membership account. We don't use tracking or advertising cookies. Stripe uses its own cookies on its payment page to prevent fraud, and the Google map on our Contact us page is provided by Google, which may set its own cookies when it loads.

Your rights

You can ask us for a copy of the information we hold about you, ask us to correct it or delete it, or object to how we use it. You can update most of your details yourself under My Membership. Email mail@buryrefs.org.uk for anything else, and we'll reply within a month.

If you're unhappy with how we've handled your information, please tell us first. You can also complain to the Information Commissioner's Office at ico.org.uk.
