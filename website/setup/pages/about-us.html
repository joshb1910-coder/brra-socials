Our story

Founded in 1898, still going strong

We began as Bury & District Referees and grew into the Bury & North Manchester Referees' Association. After joining forces with Rochdale Referees we became the Bury & Rochdale Referees' Association.

Our members referee everywhere from junior leagues on a Sunday morning to the professional game. What they share is wanting to get better and to look after each other.

How we support match officials

Monthly Development Sessions

First Thursday of the month: Laws of the Game, match clips and scenarios, with food and a catch-up afterwards.

Guest speakers

Officials and coaches from grassroots to the professional game share what it really takes.

Mentoring and wellbeing

Someone to talk to after a tough game, help with assessments and promotion, and support for new referees.

Appointments published

Members' match appointments shared every week, with the best featured on our social media.

Fitness and training events

Sessions to help you pass fitness tests and stay sharp through the season.

A community off the pitch

A members' WhatsApp community, the Christmas social and other events through the year.
