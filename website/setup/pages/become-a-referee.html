Become a referee

Thinking about refereeing? Start here.

Refereeing keeps you in the game, keeps you fit and pays for every match. You can start from 14, and you won't be doing it on your own: we've been supporting new officials in Bury, Rochdale and the surrounding area since 1898.

How to qualify

Step 1

Book the referee course

Courses are run by your County FA. Most people round here book with Manchester FA or Lancashire FA. You need to be 14 or over.

Step 2

Complete the course

A mix of online learning and practical sessions on the Laws of the Game, plus the safeguarding checks your County FA will talk you through.

Step 3

Referee your first games

Once you've qualified you'll start on local grassroots football, often youth and small-sided games, and get paid a match fee for each one.

Step 4

Join the RA

Your first season is £15. You can join before you've qualified too.

Why join the RA as a new referee?

The first season is when most new referees need someone to talk to. That's what we're here for.

  • A mentor: an experienced official you can ask about anything, from a tricky decision to your first red card.
  • Monthly Development Sessions: guest speakers and discussion of the Laws, on the first Thursday of every month.
  • Help to get games and progress: advice on getting appointed and, when you're ready, on promotion.
  • People who get it: our members' WhatsApp community, free food at meetings and socials through the season.

Questions

Do I need to have played football?

No. It helps to know the game, but the course teaches you the Laws from scratch.

How many games do I have to do?

As many as suit you. Some referees do one game a week, others do several. You tell the leagues when you're available.

I'm already qualified but new to the area. Can I join?

Of course. Come and say hello at a Development Session, or join online. If you've never been a member with us, your first season is £15.

Who do I ask if I'm not sure?

Email mail@buryrefs.org.uk and one of the committee will get back to you. We're happy to talk you through it before you book anything.
